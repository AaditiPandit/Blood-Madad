**How to read this page**

The headline tells you what needs action. Colors mean green stable, amber watch, orange warning, red critical. Click a hospital on the map or a number in the table to see its forecast and the reasons behind its risk. Use Find blood now when a patient needs units today, and Copy plan as message to send the transfer list to your team.
